# Privacy Policy for Stronghold Mastery

**Effective Date:** 3 November 2023
**Last Updated:** 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the Stronghold Mastery Discord bot (also referred to as
"Strongholds Mastery") collects, uses and protects personal data, and the rights
you have over it. It is written to comply with the UK General Data Protection
Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. It should be read
together with our
[Terms of Service](https://github.com/ghxstinthesystem/StrongholdsMasteryToS/blob/main/README.md).

This policy covers the Stronghold Mastery bot only. Any other bot or service we
operate, including our paid bot, is covered by its own terms and privacy
information.

United Kingdom, who is the **data controller** for the personal data described
in this policy ("we," "us," "our").

Contact: **StrongholdsSupport@shkbot.com**, the support server

## 2. Information We Collect

The bot stores only the Discord data its features need:

- **Discord user IDs** — where a feature needs them: war-party membership, your
  language preference, and moderation audit entries.
- **Server, channel and role IDs** — per-server configuration (welcome channel,
  ban/log channels, vacation channels, self-role setup).
- **In-game player names and player notes** — for vacation tracking and player
  notes. These are Stronghold Kingdoms identities, not Discord accounts.
- **Bot interaction records** — command usage needed to operate those features.
- **Anything else you choose to enter** through the bot's commands.

If you make a donation, we also receive the information PayPal shares with
payment recipients, typically your name, email address, the amount and the date
(see section 7).

### Where the data comes from

Most data comes from you or your server's administrators when you use the bot's
commands, and from Discord when the bot is in your server. **In-game player
names and player notes can be entered by other members of your server**, so we
may hold a name or note about you even if you have never used the bot. You have
the same rights over that data as anyone else (see section 9).

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect

- **Message content.** The bot does not request Discord's Message Content intent
  and cannot read messages in your server.
- **Presence data.** The bot does not request the Presence intent and does not
  track online status or activities.
- **Payment card or bank details.** Donations are processed entirely by PayPal.

The bot uses Discord's Server Members intent only to post configured welcome
messages when a member joins and to display current member names in command
output. Member data received this way is used for display and is not stored
beyond the IDs listed above.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions that
have legal or similarly significant effects on you, and we never sell it.

## 4. How We Use Information and Our Lawful Basis

| Purpose | Data | Lawful basis (UK GDPR Art. 6) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Running the bot's features (war parties, vacation tracking, player notes, welcome messages, self-roles, language preference) | User, server, channel and role IDs; player names and notes; interaction records | **Legitimate interests**: providing the features you and your server have chosen to use |
| Moderation tools and audit logs configured by server administrators | User IDs, moderation entries | **Legitimate interests**: helping servers keep their communities safe |
| Security, preventing abuse and enforcing our Terms (including blacklisting) | User and server IDs, interaction records | **Legitimate interests**: protecting the bot and its users |
| Responding to support, data-rights and legal requests | Your contact details and the content of your request | **Legitimate interests**, and **legal obligation** where we must respond under data protection law |
| Recording and thanking donors | Information PayPal shares with us | **Legitimate interests**: keeping accurate records of money received |
| Complying with the law or lawful requests from authorities | Any relevant data | **Legal obligation** |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have considered your interests and
rights and believe the processing is limited, expected and low-risk. You can
object at any time (see section 9).

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. We share it only:

- **With our hosting provider**, Hetzner Online GmbH, which stores the bot's
  data on servers in Germany and acts only on our instructions as a data
  processor.
- **With Discord**, as part of the bot operating on Discord's platform. Discord
  is an independent controller of the data it holds; see
  [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **With PayPal**, if you donate. PayPal is an independent controller of payment
  data; see [PayPal's Privacy Statement](https://www.paypal.com/uk/legalhub/privacy-full).
- **Where the law requires it**, or to protect the rights, safety or security of
  our users, the bot or others.
- **With your consent**, or at your direction.

## 6. International Transfers

The bot's data is stored in Germany, in the European Economic Area. The UK
recognises the EEA as providing adequate protection for personal data, so no
additional safeguards are needed for this transfer. Discord and PayPal may
process data in other countries under their own policies.

## 7. Donations

Donations are made through PayPal. We never see your card or bank details.
PayPal tells us the information it normally shares with payment recipients,
which typically includes your name, email address, the amount and the date. We
use this only to keep a record of donations, to thank you, and to respond if you
contact us about your donation. We keep donation records only for as long as we
need them for our records and any legal obligations, and we do not add donors
to any mailing list.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Feature data** (war parties, vacation entries, player notes, preferences,
  configuration) is kept while the entry remains active, which may be longer
  than 30 days. It is deleted when you or a server administrator removes it, or
  on request.
- **When the bot leaves a server**, that server's data is deleted.
- **Moderation and security records**, including blacklist entries, are kept for
  as long as they are needed to protect the bot and its users.
- **Support and data-rights correspondence** is kept for as long as needed to
  deal with the request and keep a record of how it was handled.

## 9. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
- **Rectification** — have inaccurate data corrected.
- **Erasure** — have your data deleted.
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we use your data.
- **Object** — to processing based on legitimate interests, including player
  names or notes others have entered about you.
- **Data portability** — receive data you provided in a structured,
  machine-readable format, where applicable.

These rights have some legal conditions and exceptions, for example we may keep
a blacklist entry needed to prevent abuse. To make a request, email
**StrongholdsSupport@shkbot.com** or contact us through the support server. We
may need to confirm your identity, for example by asking you to message us from
the Discord account concerned. We will respond within **one month**, and tell
you if we need longer as the law allows. Requests are free unless they are
manifestly unfounded or excessive.

### Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we
can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK
Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/
or on 0303 123 1113.

## 10. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires),
and the bot is intended for the same audience. We do not knowingly collect more
data from younger users than the features above require. If you believe a
child under 13 has had data stored by the bot, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Data Security

Stored data is encrypted at rest, at both the database level and the file level,
and access to the server is restricted to the operator. As with any online
service, no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is
100% secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights
at risk, we will notify the ICO and, where required, affected users.

## 12. Server Administrators

If you add the bot to a server, please let your members know it is in use and
point them to this policy, especially if you use player notes or moderation
logs.
